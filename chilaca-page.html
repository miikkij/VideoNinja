Description

A classic variety from Mexico. A sweet and very pleasant taste with a mild heat makes it perfect for so many dishes. Yummy!
The plant can produce a nice amount of pretty pods which ripen from dark green to dark brown, almost black. Ideal plant for outdoor / greenhouse growing.

Challenge: 1 (Easy to grow but can be a bit slow to mature)

Each pack contains approximately 8-10 or more seeds per variety.
